title: "LedgerKit: Local-first accounting runtime"
slug: ledgerkit
---

## The problem
Field teams needed to record financial activity without relying on a stable connection while keeping every journal balanced and auditable.

## The approach
I combined an embedded SQLite database compiled to WebAssembly with CRDT-based replication. Each client keeps an immutable journal and exchanges updates peer to peer.

## Outcome
Entries can be recorded, reviewed, and reconciled offline while preserving double-entry invariants across clients.

## Lessons learned
Local-first systems need clear conflict semantics in the domain model. A ledger's invariants provide a useful boundary for replication.
