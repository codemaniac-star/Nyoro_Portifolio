## The project
Cura Doula App is a perinatal support experience that helps expectant parents find a doula match and connect with certified birth-support professionals.

## My role
This was my first shipped project. It introduced the full cycle of shaping an idea, building a usable web experience, and putting it online.

## What I learned
Shipping Cura taught me how much product work happens beyond writing components: clear user journeys, readable content, responsive behavior, and completing the deployment loop.

## Next steps
The repository is an early product foundation that can continue to grow through user feedback and further iteration.
