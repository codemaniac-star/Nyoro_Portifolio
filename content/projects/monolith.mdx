## The problem
Longform architectural criticism was being published through a generic CMS that could not preserve its typographic rhythm or avoid layout shifts.

## The approach
I built a markdown-first content pipeline with typographic baseline constraints, automated ligature binding, and incremental edge compilation.

## Outcome
Cold builds fell from 4.2 minutes to 142ms. Published essays held CLS at 0.000 across an archive of more than 8,000 pieces.

## Lessons learned
Treating typography as structured content let editors make changes without breaking the reading grid.
