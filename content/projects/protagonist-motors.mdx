## The project
Protagonist Motors is a bespoke digital archive and collector’s garage logbook celebrating pure analog driving machines. Presented as a vintage editorial folio—complete with binder holes, letterpress typography, mechanical blueprints, cylinder compression diagnostics, and service ledgers—the platform treats each vehicle as an authenticated historical artifact.

## My role
Full-stack frontend and product engineering. I architected the Next.js 15 App Router experience, designed the scrapbook editorial design system with Tailwind CSS, built the interactive comparison and acquisition workflows, and integrated real-time WebRTC voice AI with ElevenLabs.

## Key features
The platform features an authenticated garage showroom of European exotics and JDM legends, granular chassis dossiers with dyno readouts and cylinder compression diagnostics, a head-to-head telemetry duel ("The Scribble Race"), a dual-currency private inspection and acquisition desk, and a real-time conversational AI archivist named J. Sterling.

## Outcome
A high-performance editorial web platform deployed on Vercel that merges analog automotive heritage with modern web performance, responsive typography, and conversational voice AI.

## Lessons learned
Balancing a richly textured visual identity (paper grains, stamps, binder margins, and mechanical blueprints) with crisp web performance, zero cumulative layout shift, and smooth WebRTC audio streaming requires disciplined component boundaries and thoughtful state isolation.
